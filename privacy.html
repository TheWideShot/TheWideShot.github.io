Privacy Policy

Claude Health MCP is a personal app used only by its owner.

  • It requests read-only access to the owner's Google Health data (profile, settings, activity, health metrics, sleep, nutrition).
  • Data is fetched to the owner's own computer and shown to the owner through the Claude desktop app. It is not sold or shared with anyone else, and it is not used for advertising.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
